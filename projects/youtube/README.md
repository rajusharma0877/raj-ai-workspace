# YouTube Documentary Project

Two distinct language channels share one production standard:
- Hindi channel: natural Hindi for an Indian audience.
- English channel: natural international English.

## Format
Original 10–15 minute illustrated historical documentaries, initially focused on ordinary people's daily lives in Ancient Rome: housing, food, work, streets, family, money, hygiene, and social differences.

## Production sequence
1. Choose a narrow episode question.
2. Build a fact-checked research ledger.
3. Write an original narrative script.
4. Create a timed visual storyboard and image/footage prompts.
5. Prepare narration, thumbnail concepts, title, description, chapters, and subtitles.
6. Human-review historical claims, rights, pronunciation, and final edit.

## Editorial rules
- Explain what is known, debated, and uncertain.
- Prefer primary sources, museums, universities, and reputable historical scholarship.
- Never fabricate quotations, citations, statistics, or source links.
- Do not copy a reference channel's scripts or distinctive packaging.
- No auto-publishing. A human approves every upload.

Output folder: `projects/youtube/output/`.
